📜 Chronicles of Night Fall Land

The Endless Night

The world is silent. The sun disappeared forty-seven years ago. Or perhaps, as ancient writings whisper, it was not the sun that disappeared—something vast covered the sky.

The Law of Puzzles

You possess no magical weapons, nor do you grow physically stronger. In Night Fall Land, your memory, logical reasoning, and observation are your only true power. Learn the rules. Question the rules. Escape the night.
